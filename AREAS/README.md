# Areas

Areas are ongoing responsibilities with no fixed completion date.

- **Health** — medical information, exercise, nutrition, and wellbeing
- **Finances** — budgeting, insurance, taxes, and financial planning
- **Career** — professional responsibilities, goals, and development
- **Learning** — ongoing study and skill development
- **Relationships** — family, friends, and social commitments
- **Home** — household maintenance, documents, and routines
- **Personal Development** — habits, values, and self-improvement
- **Digital Life** — devices, accounts, software, and digital security

Projects with a clear finish date should live in `Projects`. General reference material should live in `Resources`.
