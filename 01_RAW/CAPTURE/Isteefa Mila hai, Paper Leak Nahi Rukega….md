# Isteefa Mila hai, Paper Leak Nahi Rukega…
Source: [YT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rrYTUgC3XoY)
![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rrYTUgC3XoY)

Minister Resigned. The Paper Leak Machine Didn't.  
  
Everyone thinks Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation is the story here, a big win for the students who protested NEET 2026. But the real story is what happens now that the headlines move on.  
  
We go back through five years of solved paper leak cases, 48 leaks across 16 states, over 1.5 crore candidates affected, to map exactly where in the system these leaks actually happen, from recruitment exams to NEET to school board papers.  
The people getting arrested are rarely outsiders. They are teachers, government officials, printing press staff, and repeat masterminds who keep resurfacing case after case. And out of 148 documented cases since 2015, only one has ended in a conviction.  
  
This is not about who holds the ministry. It is about whether the incentive to leak a paper ever actually goes away, and what has to change in printing, technology, investigation and accountability for that to happen.  
  
A resignation makes headlines for a day. A broken system keeps costing students years.
