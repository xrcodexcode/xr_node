## 03. Chronological Exile Timeline (1985–1997)

1. **1985**: Ouster from Apple; summer reflection in Paris; founding of NeXT; poaching lawsuit.
2. **1986**: Retreat filmed for PBS *Entrepreneurs*; acquisition of Pixar from George Lucas ($5M); Paul Rand logo design ($100k).
3. **1986–1987**: Ross Perot $20M investment; Fremont automated factory construction; casing and paint yield crises.
4. **1988–1989**: IBM $60M NeXTStep licensing deal; $10,500 NeXT Cube launch; Canon $100M investment for 16.67% stake.
5. **1990–1991**: Walking out on Dallas IBM meeting; Ross Perot departure; Andy Grove audit; $10M channel stuffing debt exposure.
6. **1992–1994**: Canon $20M emergency loan; closure of hardware factory; pivot to software; first profitable year ($1M net).
7. **1995–1996**: WebObjects launch; Michael Dell e-commerce partnership; Pixar IPO; Garrett Rice cold-call pitch to Apple.
8. **1996–1997**: Live software presentation duel against BeOS; Apple acquires NeXT for $400M; Steve Jobs returns to refound Apple.

## 04. Key Themes & Analytical Frameworks

- **Startup Financial Discipline**: Contrast between scarcity-driven focus at early Apple vs. abundance-driven waste at early NeXT.
- **Leadership & Cultural Maturity**: Evolution from a volatile micromanager into an empowering executive who retains top-tier talent.
- **Product Demonstration vs. Hype**: The decisive role of live working software over reputation during corporate M&A.
